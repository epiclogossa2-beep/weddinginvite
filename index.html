Dress Code

What to wear

We want you to feel comfortable, celebrated and beautifully dressed for the day. We kindly ask that guests avoid wearing white, as it is reserved for the bride, and that gentlemen avoid black suits. Rich autumn tones, earthy neutrals and soft golds would be perfect, and we can't wait to see you all dressed up!

Ladies

Any colour except white or ivory. Sage, olive, terracotta, mocha, dusty rose and gold will look stunning.

Gentlemen

Any suit except black. Navy, charcoal, olive, brown and tan are all lovely.
